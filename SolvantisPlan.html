Founder experience

Twenty years of lessons from the shop floor to the warehouse.

Mark Mamrot founded Monsterthreads with his business and life partner, Charintorn Pongpaet, in 2004 while studying at UNSW. Its idea was “art for every day”: putting favourite artworks on the things people use, including T-shirts, homewares and accessories.

Monsterthreads grew into a leading gift destination spanning both retail and wholesale. Immediately before COVID, the business operated ten stores, giving Mark direct experience across buying, inventory, stores, staffing, customer service, ecommerce, wholesale and distribution.

Solvantis turns more than 20 years of practical retail and wholesale experience into a platform that understands how traders actually work.

That experience shapes the product: connected workflows, less repeated administration, clearer information and tools designed to save operators time. Solvantis is not software looking for a retail use case; it is the operating platform Mark wanted while building and running a complex commerce business.
