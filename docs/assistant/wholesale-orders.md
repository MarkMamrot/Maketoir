# Wholesale ordering and account access

The wholesale portal shows the approved catalogue, pricing, availability, account details, saved lists, drafts, and orders available to the signed-in company member and assigned buying location.

## Catalogue and pricing

Catalogue results use current product availability, approved brand access, wholesale pricing, stock, and indent policy. Historical order prices do not replace current catalogue pricing when reordering.

## Drafts and submitted orders

Drafts remain editable until submitted. Submitted sales orders are read-only in the buyer portal and show buyer-safe lifecycle, product, fulfilment, terms, and total information. Internal supplier notes are never shown.

## Account and location boundaries

Orders and drafts are restricted to the active contact, company, member, and buying-location identity. A member cannot inspect another company's orders or switch location unless that capability is explicitly supported for the account.

## Reordering

Reordering rebuilds a cart using current catalogue data. Retired or unavailable lines may be omitted, stock-limited lines may be reduced, and current pricing and indent rules always apply.
