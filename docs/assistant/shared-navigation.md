# Finding your way around Solvantis

Solvantis shows tools according to the signed-in user's role and the part of the platform they are using. If a screen or action is unavailable, check that the user is signed in to the correct workspace and has the required access before changing the workflow.

## Getting help for the current screen

Ask about the outcome you need, including the order, product, location, or account involved when appropriate. Solvantis can explain the supported workflow and, where permission allows, inspect current read-only business information. It will not make changes on your behalf.

## When a workflow differs

Some outcomes have a specific supported path to protect stock, financial records, or account ownership. A different sequence is not necessarily an error. Solvantis should explain the supported alternative and confirm whether it meets the essential business requirement.
