# Getting Started and Account Approval

Use this guide to apply to a supplier's wholesale portal and sign in after that supplier approves your account.

## Main operations

- Submit accurate company and contact details.
- Open the verification email and confirm the application.
- Wait for the supplier to approve or reject the request.
- Sign in with the approved business email and six-digit code.
- Contact the supplier when approval or email delivery needs human review.

## At a glance

| What you see | Application or login state | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Check your email** after applying | Email verification is pending | Open the verification link within its valid period |
| **Your email is verified** | Application is waiting for supplier review | Wait for the approval or rejection email |
| Approval email | Account, initial location, and Owner access are active | Return to the supplier portal and request a sign-in code |
| Rejection email | Access was not approved | Contact the supplier if circumstances have changed |
| **Enter your code** | A sign-in attempt was requested | Enter the six-digit email code |
| Generic sign-in message but no email arrives | The email may be unapproved, inactive, delayed, or mistyped | Check the address and spam folder, then contact the supplier |

## Before you begin

- [ ] Use a business email address you can open immediately.
- [ ] Have the legal or trading company name and contact name ready.
- [ ] Enter an ABN as 11 digits when supplied.
- [ ] Read and accept the application consent statement.
- [ ] Use the correct supplier's portal; approval applies to that supplier.

> **Note:** Submitting and verifying an application does not grant immediate catalogue access. The supplier must approve it before the email can sign in as an active wholesale buyer.

## Step-by-step

### Apply and verify

1. On the supplier sign-in page, select **Apply for wholesale access**.
2. Enter the company name, contact name, business email, and any optional phone, ABN, or message.
3. Confirm the consent statement and select **Submit application**.
4. Open the verification email and use its link. If it has expired, submit the application again to receive a new link.
5. Wait while the supplier reviews the verified application.

### Sign in after approval

1. Return to the same supplier portal.
2. Enter the approved business email and select **Email me a sign-in code**.
3. Open the latest email and enter its six-digit code.
4. Select **Open wholesale portal**.
5. Check the company and buying location shown in the header before ordering.

## Approval and access decisions

| Situation | What you should do |
|---|---|
| You applied but did not verify the email | Use the verification email; the supplier cannot review an unverified request |
| The verification link expired | Submit the application again for a new link |
| The supplier has not decided yet | Wait or contact the supplier's support address; repeated applications do not bypass review |
| You were approved but cannot receive a code | Confirm the exact approved email and ask the supplier to check that the contact and membership remain active |
| A colleague needs access | Ask an account Owner or Admin to add their already approved wholesale email from Account |

> **Important:** Sign-in responses do not confirm whether an email exists. This protects wholesale accounts from address probing, so the supplier is the correct contact for unresolved access questions.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Verification email is missing | Delivery delay, spam filtering, or a mistyped address | Check spam, confirm the address, then resubmit once if needed |
| Verification link says expired | Its valid period ended | Submit a fresh application |
| Sign-in code is rejected | It is mistyped, expired, already used, or not the newest code | Request another code and enter the latest six digits |
| Portal returns to sign-in | The approved contact, company, membership, or location is no longer active | Contact the supplier to review account access |
| Catalogue is empty after sign-in | The account has no approved brands/products, or filters hide them | Clear filters and ask the supplier to review catalogue access |

## Worked examples

### New retailer application

Harbour Homewares submits its company and buyer details on Monday, then verifies the email from the same inbox. The portal confirms that the supplier will review the request. After the approval email arrives, the buyer returns to the portal, requests a six-digit code, signs in, and sees only Harbour Homewares' approved catalogue and buying location.
