# Business Intelligence and Inventory Analysis

## Main operations

- Maintain Business Key Information and Brand Profile as governed context.
- Refresh supported source data before relying on time-sensitive analysis.
- Use reports and inventory analysis as decision support, then inspect source records and definitions.
- Keep generated analysis distinct from verified facts and authorised operational changes.

## Dashboard and AI Helper

Dashboard summarises reporting and activity. Open the owning analysis before deciding from an aggregate value. AI Helper uses the business context available to its workflow; ordinary chat does not approve a plan, publish content, or execute an external change.

## Business context and data

Business Key Information and Brand Profile provide maintained context for analysis. Sync Data refreshes supported sources, while calculated views derive bounded measures from available data. Review source freshness, reporting window, and measure definitions when comparing results.

## Inventory analysis

Inactive Candidates, Possible Losses, Space Efficiency, and Stock Turnover identify records for review. Inspect source products, location stock, sales windows, incoming and committed quantities, and the stated assumptions before changing replenishment, pricing, or merchandising.

## Troubleshooting

- Refresh or inspect source status when an analysis appears stale.
- Align date range, location, and calculation definition before comparing views.
- Treat a candidate flag as a prompt to investigate, not proof that a product should be removed.

## Worked examples

### Investigate declining stock turnover

Open Stock Turnover, select the relevant period and product, and inspect sales and stock context. Compare related inventory analysis and confirm source freshness before deciding on replenishment, pricing, or merchandising.

### Review an inactive candidate

Open Inactive Candidates, inspect the product's sales window, current and incoming stock, location coverage, and seasonality. Follow the source records before changing product status or ordering.
