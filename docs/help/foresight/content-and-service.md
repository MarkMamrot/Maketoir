# Brand Assets, Website, and Customer Service

## Main operations

- Maintain reusable Models, Backdrops, Poses, Scenes, and Templates with appropriate source rights.
- Review generated website copy and candidate imagery before publication.
- Use pending and bulk listing views to coordinate controlled content updates.
- Review customer context and every draft before sending a response.
- Maintain connection and marketing settings required by the active workflow.

## Brand assets

Brand Assets provides governed inputs for reusable creative work. Confirm source imagery, usage rights, intended role, and output quality. A generated candidate is not automatically an approved customer-facing asset.

## Website content

Website Content Studio, Web Field Templates, pending-online work, and bulk listing tools prepare product content for review. Existing images remain intact unless a user explicitly changes them. Generated descriptions and research require review for accuracy, tone, product fit, and required claims.

## Customer service

Customer Service Inbox, Compose, and Templates support customer-query handling and drafts. Review the customer, source conversation, order context, tone, factual claims, and recipients before sending. Features marked as coming soon are not available workflows.

## Settings and connections

Appearance controls supported presentation. Connections owns service authorization and status. Marketing Settings and Data Source settings control applicable analysis, alerts, guardrails, and inputs. Available changes depend on role.

## Troubleshooting

- If generation lacks context, confirm the relevant brand asset, product data, and template are selected.
- If publication is unavailable, check review state, required fields, role, and connection status.
- If a service draft lacks order context, inspect the source customer and order before editing the reply.
- Reconnect expired authorization before rerunning dependent work.

## Worked examples

### Prepare a product listing

Open the product in Website Content Studio, review source data and the selected field template, generate or edit the proposed copy, inspect candidate imagery without replacing existing images, and approve only the content ready for publication.

### Reply to a customer query

Open the Inbox item, verify the customer and related order context, prepare or edit the draft, check recipients and factual claims, and send only after human review.
