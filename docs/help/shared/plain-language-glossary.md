# Plain-language Glossary

Use this glossary when a status, quantity, or document name is unfamiliar.

## Main operations

- Look up the exact word shown on screen.
- Check the "What it does not mean" column before acting.
- Open the product-specific guide for the full workflow.
- Ask an administrator or support when the displayed term and real situation do not match.

## Stock and fulfilment

| Term | Plain meaning | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock on hand | Units currently recorded at a location | Units free for every new order |
| Committed stock | Units set aside for existing demand | Units already shipped |
| Available stock | Stock on hand less quantities already committed under the workflow | A promise that every unit is saleable without checking |
| Incoming stock | Units expected from open purchasing work | Stock already received |
| Allocation | Linking available or incoming units to an order need | Shipping or receiving the units |
| Fulfilment | Supplying ordered items to the customer | Taking payment by itself |
| Backorder | Customer demand that cannot yet be supplied from available stock | A cancelled order |
| Indent or pre-order | A supplier-approved quantity ordered for future supply under that workflow | Stock currently on hand |
| Branch transfer | Moving stock from one business location to another | A customer sale or supplier purchase |
| Stocktake | Comparing counted units with the recorded quantity | An automatic explanation for every difference |

## Orders, returns, and customer value

| Term | Plain meaning | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase order | An order sent to or prepared for a supplier | Stock received or a supplier bill paid |
| Sales order | A record of customer demand and fulfilment | Proof that every item shipped or every payment cleared |
| Credit note | A document that records returned or reduced value | Always a cash refund |
| Store credit | Customer value kept with the business for future use | Cash in the till or a bank refund |
| Gift card | Prepaid customer value issued under the gift-card workflow | The same balance as store credit |
| Refund | Returning value through an appropriate payment method | Automatically adding stock back |
| Reconciliation | Checking that records from two places agree and explaining differences | Silently changing one side to match the other |

## Planning and connected services

| Term | Plain meaning | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | Information shown in support of a finding or recommendation | Proof that the recommendation is correct |
| Recommendation | A suggested action for a person to review | Approval or completed work |
| Accepted version | The exact plan, creative item, or design a person approved | Approval of later edits |
| Implementation | A record of what was actually changed | The same as approving a plan |
| Experiment control | The current or comparison version | A version that cannot change outside the accepted design |
| Experiment treatment | The version being tested against the control | A guaranteed improvement |
| Connected | The service accepted access for the selected account | Every mapping and workflow is ready |
| Sync | Moving or refreshing supported information between systems | A guarantee that every record matched |

> **Tip:** A document status and a real-world event are different. For example, an accepted plan is not a launched campaign, and an open purchase order is not received stock.

## Worked examples

### Available versus on hand

A store has 10 units on hand and 4 committed to existing orders. Available stock is 6 for the workflow using those commitments; the 4 committed units are still physically present until fulfilled.

### Credit note versus refund

A returned $110 jacket may create a $110 credit note to record the reduced customer value. The refund could be store credit, card, cash, or a supported combination. The credit note and refund describe related but different parts of the return.
