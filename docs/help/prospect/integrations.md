# Integration Approach

Native Xero capability supports retail accounting, payment clearing and reconciliation workflows. Native Shopify capability covers supported product, inventory, order, customer, return and loyalty flows across separately configured store connections. Cin7 capability supports product, stock and purchasing alignment with clear ownership between systems.

Amazon Australia sales-channel workflows are available where configured and ready. Supported scope includes seller-fulfilled orders and existing-ASIN offer mapping and publication. Seller-account authorization alone does not activate synchronization; readiness checks must pass. This is not a blanket offer of FBA, international marketplaces or full new-catalogue creation.

Configured Australia Post eParcel services support shipping quotes, parcels, labels, tracking and manifests within fulfilment workflows. This does not imply MyPost Business or every carrier service. Supported Zeller terminal integration connects EFTPOS at POS checkout.

Google business tools, Google Ads, Google Analytics and Meta Ads can provide connected communications, marketing context and analysis where the relevant offering is enabled. Available analysis and governed marketing workflows vary by plan and implementation scope.

Scale and Enterprise include supported standard 3PL workflows, including agreed order hand-off, fulfilment status and inventory synchronization patterns where the provider is supported. Solvantis confirms provider fit and intended workflow during onboarding. Provider-specific connector development, third-party fees, complex warehouse routing and specialist external platforms may be separately scoped and quoted.
