# Growth Plan

Growth is for a single-store retailer or wholesaler with annual turnover up to A$2 million. It includes one location, two registers, up to 10 users and three standard integrations.

## Price and term

Growth is monthly. It is advertised from $159 per month with eligible Connected Payments volume, or $199 per month without Connected Payments. Prices are AUD excluding GST. The three-month $1 introductory offer applies to Starter, not Growth.

## Included capabilities

Growth includes the complete operational feature set, the native online shop and the wholesale portal. It includes self onboarding, import templates and a local business-hours helpdesk.

AI Automation uses separately purchased credits at preferred rates. Standard reports and non-generative calculations do not consume credits.

Growth does not include a custom online-shop domain. Custom domains are included on Core, Scale and Enterprise.
