# Plans, Pricing and Current Offer

Starter is for businesses with annual turnover up to A$600,000. It provides one location, one register, three users, one standard integration, the complete operational retail foundation and the native online shop. The displayed price is from $59 per month with eligible Connected Payments volume; the price without Connected Payments is $99 per month.

Core is for businesses with annual turnover up to A$5 million. It is $349 per month on a 12-month agreement billed monthly and includes three locations, five registers, 15 users, three standard integrations, the complete operational feature set, the native online shop and the wholesale portal.

Scale is for businesses with annual turnover of A$5 million or more. It is $749 per month on a 12-month agreement billed monthly and includes the same operational feature set as Core, with 10 locations, 20 registers, 50 users, eight standard integrations, higher order capacity, supported 3PL workflows, White Glove onboarding and priority local support.

Enterprise starts from $1,999 per month on an annual scoped contract. It includes contracted capacity, supported 3PL workflows, dedicated app and data infrastructure, White Glove onboarding, bulk AI credit rates, a dedicated account lead, an SLA and 100 governed customisation hours per contract year. Complex infrastructure, provider-specific connectors, third-party charges, enhanced support or accelerated customisation may increase the quote.

AI Automation is available on every plan. Generative and agentic actions use separately purchased credits. Starter pays standard credit rates, Core receives preferred rates, Scale receives volume-preferred rates and Enterprise receives contracted bulk rates. Standard reports, stored dashboards and non-generative calculations do not consume credits.

The public introductory offer is 3 months of Starter for a one-time $1 payment. Normal Starter pricing applies after the offer period unless the service is cancelled beforehand. All prices are AUD excluding GST.
