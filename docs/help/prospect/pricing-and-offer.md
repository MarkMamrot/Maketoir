# Plans, Pricing and Current Offer

Basic provides the core retail platform, point of sale, loyalty, AI workflow assistance, and included Shopify and Xero capability for smaller teams. Growth adds advanced inventory, planning, analytics, CRM and wholesale capability for multi-location retailers. Enterprise provides tailored commercial terms, priority service and discovery for custom platform or 3PL needs.

The public introductory offer is 3 months of the chosen plan for a one-time $1 payment. No payment card is required upfront and there is no lock-in. Normal plan billing applies after the offer period unless the service is cancelled beforehand.
