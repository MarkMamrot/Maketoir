# Retail Use Cases

A single-store retailer can combine core inventory, purchasing, point of sale, loyalty and accounting capability. A growing multi-location retailer can coordinate product information, stock visibility, transfers, replenishment, customer activity and reporting across the group.

Retailers selling in stores, through multiple Shopify stores, through configured Amazon Australia seller accounts, through a Solvantis online shop or to wholesale customers can bring supported channel activity into a more consistent operating view. Each Shopify connection has its own settings; the native shop does not depend on Shopify. Fit for unusual fulfilment, specialist platforms or highly tailored logistics is confirmed through discovery.

## Daily store work

Store Daybooks support opening, daily, weekly and closing sign-offs, staff notices with read acknowledgements, customer follow-up, incidents and product references. Store supplies and stock requests can be followed through warehouse packing, sending and receiving. Daybook requests coordinate work; they do not themselves record sales or move inventory. Stock movement uses the relevant inventory workflow.

Supported ordinary POS checkout can continue while disconnected and sync later. Online-dependent actions, including loyalty and return workflows, require a connection.

## Repeat wholesale orders

Approved wholesale buyers can access their catalogue and pricing, prepare draft baskets, use saved lists and repeat orders, submit orders and review history. The wholesale portal is included on Growth, Core, Scale and Enterprise. Customer terms and indent ordering depend on configuration.
