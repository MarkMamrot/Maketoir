# Retail Use Cases

A single-store retailer can combine core inventory, purchasing, point of sale, loyalty and accounting capability. A growing multi-location retailer can coordinate product information, stock visibility, transfers, replenishment, customer activity and reporting across the group.

Retailers selling in stores, through Shopify, through a Solvantis online shop or to wholesale customers can bring supported channel activity into a more consistent operating view. Fit for unusual fulfilment, specialist platforms or highly tailored logistics is confirmed through discovery.
