# CRM Segments, Tasks, and Pipeline

Use CRM to turn current customer information into a practical follow-up list without changing sales, credits, or loyalty records.

## Main operations

- Filter eligible customers and leads in the CRM contact list.
- Create and complete dated follow-up tasks.
- Build live segments from current contact and commerce data.
- Track forecast opportunities through open, won, and lost stages.
- Open the customer profile before recording the next action.

## At a glance

| Tool | Use it for | It does not do |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Find customers and leads by tag, follow-up, or last touch | Change source sales or balances |
| Tasks | Assign a specific follow-up with due date and priority | Move an opportunity automatically |
| Segments | Recalculate a group from saved rules | Freeze a permanent mailing list |
| Pipeline | Forecast a possible tax-inclusive AUD sale | Create an invoice, order, or payment |
| Customer profile | Review activity and record interactions | Replace the linked transaction |

## Before you begin

- [ ] Search for the person or company and remove obvious duplicates first.
- [ ] Confirm the contact is an eligible lead or customer type.
- [ ] Decide whether you need a one-off task, a reusable segment, or a sales opportunity.
- [ ] Keep notes factual and suitable for a shared business record.

> **Important:** Pipeline value is a forecast, not booked revenue. Winning an opportunity does not create a Sales Order or accounting entry.

## Step-by-step

### Create and complete a follow-up task

1. Open **Contacts > CRM** and find the customer.
2. Open the customer profile and select **Tasks**.
3. Enter a clear title, due date, priority, assignee, and useful detail.
4. Save the task.
5. After the follow-up, record a factual interaction if needed.
6. Mark the task completed, or cancel it if the action is no longer required.

### Build a live segment

1. Open **CRM > Segments** and choose **New segment**.
2. Name the segment and choose only the rules needed: contact type, tags, revenue source, revenue range, recent activity, inactivity, location, or loyalty status.
3. Choose whether revenue comes from POS, Sales Orders, or both.
4. Save the segment and choose **View** to calculate current membership.
5. Review representative customers before exporting the displayed results.
6. Refresh later to recalculate membership from current data.

### Track an opportunity

1. Open **CRM > Pipeline** and choose **Opportunity**.
2. Select a lead or customer, stage, title, expected tax-inclusive AUD value, probability, owner, and next-action date.
3. Save the opportunity and create a task when someone must follow up.
4. Move the opportunity as the real conversation progresses.
5. When moving a lead to **Won**, choose whether the lead becomes a retail or B2B customer.
6. When moving an opportunity to **Lost**, record a useful reason where appropriate.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A task is missing from the queue | Search, due, priority, or assignee filter | Clear filters and check the customer profile |
| Segment membership changed | Live rules were recalculated from newer data | Review the rules and current customer activity |
| A customer does not match a location rule | No qualifying POS activity at that location | Check the source sale and selected location |
| A lead cannot be marked won | Conversion choice is missing or invalid | Choose retail or B2B when prompted |
| Pipeline total seems too low | It uses expected value multiplied by probability for open opportunities | Review each open opportunity's value and probability |

## Worked examples

### Build a lapsed-customer follow-up list

Create a segment for retail customers with combined POS and Sales Order revenue who have been inactive for 90 days. View the current matches, open a customer profile to confirm the history, then assign follow-up tasks to the appropriate staff member.

### Understand weighted pipeline value

An open $1,100 opportunity at 50% probability contributes $550 to weighted open value. The $1,100 is tax-inclusive forecast value; neither amount is a sale until staff create and complete the normal sales workflow.
