# Sales Orders and Fulfilment

Use Sales Orders to record customer demand and reduce stock only when goods are actually shipped.

## Main operations

- Create and review a Draft order.
- Confirm the order when customer demand is real.
- Choose **Display Fields** to show the order details needed for the current task.
- **Save dimensions** beneath each ready order, then batch only measured orders.
- Choose postage inside the batch, create labels and download them.
- Use **Prepare**, **Batches**, and **Manifests** to keep packing, saved working groups, and carrier lodgement separate.
- Prepare one parcel with all remaining quantities automatically; expand item allocation only when splitting parcels.
- Add, remove, move, or **Undo batch** without deleting saved dimensions or cancelling existing postage.
- Edit an eligible shipment's parcel details, delivery address and service before dispatch.
- Close dispatched shipments into a carrier manifest and print the lodgement summary.
- Fulfil only the quantities sent to the customer.
- Review each line's on-hand, ready, protected incoming, shortfall, and demand priority before fulfilment.
- Continue a partial fulfilment or resolve the remainder.
- Move selected outstanding quantities and their chosen protected incoming supply to a new or compatible existing Sales Order.
- Review actual shipped COGS separately from any current-cost estimate for quantities not yet shipped.

## At a glance

| Stage or choice                          | What it means                                                                                  | Stock effect                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft                                    | The order is still being prepared                                                              | No shipment; stock is not reduced                         |
| Confirmed                                | Customer demand is active                                                                      | Quantity can be committed, but stock on hand is unchanged |
| Save dimensions                          | Saves packed measurements and remaining-item allocations before batching                       | No postage purchase; stock is unchanged                   |
| Add to batch                             | Groups orders whose parcel dimensions have already been saved                                  | No postage purchase; stock is unchanged                   |
| Save postage service                     | Saves the reviewed service and quote inside the batch                                          | No postage purchase; stock is unchanged                   |
| Undo batch                               | Removes the working group while keeping dimensions and any existing postage                     | No cancellation or stock movement                         |
| Submit to Australia Post & create labels | Creates billable Australia Post shipments and generates printable labels                       | Postage is charged; stock is unchanged                    |
| Mark dispatched                          | Records the parcel quantities that physically left and sends tracking to the connected channel | Shipped quantities reduce stock                           |
| Create booking & manifest                | Seals selected dispatched shipments into one carrier pickup batch                              | No additional stock movement                              |
| Fulfil order                             | Ship every outstanding quantity and complete the order                                          | Shipped quantities reduce stock                           |
| Partially fulfil now                     | Ship entered quantities and leave the balance on this order                                    | Only the shipped quantity reduces stock                   |
| Create a backorder for the remainder      | Ship entered quantities and move the supply shortfall to a separate Backordered Sales Order     | Only the shipped quantity reduces stock                   |
| Complete                                 | All intended shipments or remainder decisions are finished                                     | No extra movement beyond recorded shipments               |

## Before you begin

- [ ] Confirm the customer, delivery location, stock location, products, quantities, and tax-inclusive selling prices.
- [ ] Check whether incoming stock is already protected for this order.
- [ ] Count or verify the goods being dispatched.
- [ ] Configure an active Australia Post eParcel account under **Settings > Shipping**.
- [ ] Record variant weights and dimensions for products that will be packed automatically.
- [ ] For international orders, complete each product's customs description, HS code, country of origin, and dangerous or restricted goods setting.
- [ ] Make sure you are not using an advisor account, which is read-only.

> **Important:** Record what physically ships. If a shipment succeeds but a later accounting action fails, retry the unfinished accounting action; do not fulfil the goods again.

## Choose list fields

Select **Display Fields** above the Sales Orders list to choose its visible columns. **SO #** and **Customer** remain visible and frozen while the rest of the table scrolls horizontally. Your selection is saved for the current business.

**Channel Order #** shows the order reference supplied by Shopify or another connected sales channel, while **SO #** remains the Solvantis order number. **Shipping Method** preserves the delivery or pickup method supplied by the connected channel. Search matches customer names, Solvantis order numbers and connected-channel order references. Other optional fields include channel, location, status, dates, payment details, totals, notes and delivery address.

For Shopify orders, Solvantis copies the shipping address supplied on the order into the Sales Order delivery fields when the order is created, paid, updated or imported. Shopify pickup or other orders without a shipping address may correctly leave those fields blank.

When Shopify sends an edited order update while the Solvantis order is Draft or Confirmed, Solvantis uses the current Shopify quantities and removes lines that no longer have any quantity. Removed items are not kept alongside replacement items. Once fulfilment has begun, channel updates do not replace the order lines or rewrite recorded stock movements. Review the remaining quantities before packing or dispatching.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Sales > Sales Orders** and select **New Sales Order**.
2. Choose the customer and location, then add the products and ordered quantities.
3. Review prices, tax treatment, early-payment discount, freight, dates, and notes. Save the order as Draft while it is still being prepared.
4. Confirm the order when the customer demand is ready to proceed. If recorded stock cannot cover the order, review **Stock sourcing** before confirmation. Solvantis clearly identifies items with no stock currently available and shows the ordered quantity, stock available now, the quantity needing incoming supply, and eligible Confirmed or Partially Received Purchase Orders at the same location. Each supply option shows a clickable PO number, supplier, expected date, total line quantity ordered and received, free quantity available to allocate, and the quantity to protect for this Sales Order. Suggested quantities use the earliest expected Purchase Orders first, but no incoming stock is protected until you choose **Confirm & Allocate**. Reduce or remove a suggestion when it should supply another order. If any shortage remains, acknowledge each unsourced quantity before choosing **Confirm with Unsourced Quantity**, or choose **Return to Draft** without changing the order.
5. Open **Shipping Workspace** and select **Prepare**. **Orders ready to ship** shows eligible Confirmed or In Progress delivery orders, including measured orders not yet assigned to a batch. Pickup orders do not enter the carrier queue. Orders already grouped or booked with the carrier must use their existing shipment rather than being prepared again.
6. Choose the carrier account, then enter length, width and height in centimetres and packed weight in kilograms directly beneath each order's details. An optional preset supplies starting dimensions; confirm actual packed measurements and weight, including packaging. The account's dispatch location and order's delivery address must be complete before preparation can be saved.
7. A new single parcel automatically contains all remaining unshipped quantities, not the original ordered quantities. **Show contents** displays a read-only summary rather than editable SKU quantity fields beside measurements. Use **Split into another parcel** when several parcels are needed. **Allocate items between parcels** distributes existing quantities; totals must remain unchanged. Removing a parcel moves its contents to the first remaining parcel: recheck measurements and weight. Fractional quantities remain supported. Warnings identify the affected item and required total. Unmatched Shopify lines retain their original title alongside **SHOPIFY-MISC** where available.
8. For an international order, review its unframed **Customs declaration** section. Choose Sale, Gift, Sample, or Return. The declaration shows each allocated product and SKU, customs description, HS code, country of origin, allocated quantity, and declared unit and total value in AUD. Sale values are calculated from the allocated Sales Order quantity using the discounted selling price and the order's tax treatment, and cannot be edited. Gift, Sample, and Return values begin with that sale value as a reference; edit them when needed and explicitly confirm that the non-sale values are complete and accurate.
9. Resolve every product-specific customs message before continuing. A missing customs description, invalid or missing HS code, missing country of origin, missing positive weight, or dangerous or restricted goods setting blocks pricing and preparation. Use **Review customs** to show the product declarations and **Update product customs in Products** to open product setup. Dangerous or restricted goods cannot be overridden in Shipping Workspace.
10. Select **Save dimensions** under each order. Measurements, item allocations and required customs declarations are saved before batching and remain available after closing or refreshing, including to other staff. **Dimensions saved - ready for batch** enables the order's checkbox. Editing dimensions disables selection until saved again and clears any previous postage choice. Reload an order changed by another staff member rather than overwriting it.
11. Select measured orders and choose **New batch** or an existing working group. Use **Create batch with selected orders** or **Add selected orders to batch**. Unmeasured or unsaved orders cannot join a batch. Inside **Batches**, use **Get prices & choose postage** for each order without a service. Select **Get shipping prices**, review Australia Post contract services and GST-inclusive quotes, choose one, then **Save postage service**. Saved delivery and parcel details are available in the disclosure. Saving dimensions, grouping orders and choosing a service do not buy postage or reduce stock. Switching tabs retains unsaved preparation; closing or leaving asks before discarding it.
12. After services are chosen, **Stock available for this batch** confirms that all required stock is on hand. **Recheck stock** repeats the check without adjusting stock. Solvantis checks packed quantities together at each fulfilment branch, so one available unit cannot cover two orders. A shortage displays **Stock shortage - review before buying labels** with affected orders, SKU, required and on-hand quantities, purchase-order incoming quantity, and outstanding Sent or Partially Received transfers. Incoming stock is not on hand until received. Use **Receive transfer** when it has arrived, or **Start stock adjustment** for a blank draft stocktake preloaded with shortage products when a physical recount is needed. Recheck after resolving the shortage. Buying labels despite a shortage requires explicit acknowledgement; dispatch checks stock again.
13. Review the saved services and prices, then select **Submit to Australia Post & create labels** and confirm the billable action. Solvantis repeats the stock-readiness check and rechecks each selected service and price immediately before submission. Long street-address text is wrapped across Australia Post's address lines without truncation. If one consignment is rejected, the other consignments continue and retain their successful postage and labels; correct only the named failed consignment and reopen the batch. If a price changed or the service became unavailable, use **Edit shipment details** where available, refresh prices and review the changed service before saving. Never silently accept a different price.
14. Open each available label PDF and print it. Solvantis keeps Parcel Post, Express Post, and International labels in separate label requests because they use different layouts. Parcel Post uses Australia Post's four-label A4 layout, Express Post uses its three-label A4 layout, and **International labels** use one label per A4 page. A mixed-service preparation batch therefore produces separate PDFs that must be printed on the matching stock. Labels include both the Solvantis SO number and the connected channel order number when the channel supplies one. Use **Check label status** when Australia Post is still generating a label.
15. After the labelled parcels physically leave, select **Mark dispatched**. Solvantis fulfills the quantities assigned to those parcels, reduces stock, and marks the Sales Order In Progress when a quantity remains or Completed when every line is shipped. Australia Post tracking appears under **Shipments & Tracking** in the Sales Order detail. Solvantis sends every parcel number and tracking link to the original supported sales channel. Shopify orders receive the matching fulfillment with Australia Post tracking; if carrier tracking is not yet available, the channel update remains pending rather than creating an untracked fulfillment. A channel error leaves the shipment at **Channel sync pending** without repeating stock movement; reopen it and select **Mark dispatched** to retry only the channel sync.
16. Open the **Manifests** tab in Shipping Workspace. Dispatched shipments are grouped by carrier account and dispatch location so each group represents one physical pickup origin.
17. Select the shipments going in the pickup and choose **Create booking & manifest**. For Australia Post this creates one carrier Order and seals those shipments so they cannot enter another manifest.
18. Manifest history lists the included channel or Solvantis order references. Choose **Print Parcel labels**, **Print Express labels**, or **Print International labels** to retrieve a PDF containing only that label layout, or choose **Print manifest** to print and sign the Australia Post Order Summary. These documents can be printed again from manifest history. International labels are never combined with Parcel Post or Express Post labels.

### Manage workspace batches

- **Prepare** saves dimensions directly under each ready order. Select saved, measured orders for a new or existing batch. Booked **Unbatched shipments** remain separate so their postage cannot be purchased twice.
- **Batches** lists saved working groups with order/parcel counts, preparation stage, quoted cost, and available recorded carrier charges. Open a batch to see its orders and continue its next step.
- **Add orders** returns to ready orders with that batch selected as the destination. Save missing dimensions before adding orders; choose postage after batching.
- **Remove from batch** or a bulk move preserves measurements, allocations, addresses, services and postage. Unpurchased preparations return to **Orders ready to ship** with dimensions retained. Booked shipments remain in **Unbatched shipments**.
- **Undo batch** removes the whole working group, not its shipments. It preserves dimensions and any existing postage. It does not cancel labels or reverse dispatch. Confirm before proceeding; unsafe or dispatched shipments block it.
- Grouping changes do not cancel or buy postage, change ordered products or prices, or update stock. A shipment with existing postage remains booked even when unbatched.
- An affected shared PDF must be regenerated if it would otherwise print shipments outside the requested batch. Discard its older copies and generate current labels before dispatch. Valid individual labels can remain available when grouping alone does not make the PDF unsafe.
- Dispatched or manifested shipments cannot move between working groups. In-progress or uncertain operations must be resolved first. A shared PDF containing a dispatched or manifested member can also prevent a move.
- **Manifests** records the later carrier lodgement/pickup, not the internal working group. Carrier-account and pickup-location restrictions still apply.

### Edit shipment details before dispatch

1. Open **Shipping Workspace > Batches**, open the saved batch and select **Edit shipment details** for the individual order. Unbatched shipments have the same editing action in **Prepare**.
2. Review the exact saved measurements and quantities. A single parcel shows its saved contents read-only; multi-parcel editing exposes allocation separately from measurements. Saved allocations are not replaced with new order defaults. Change dimensions, packed weights, distribution of existing quantities, delivery address or carrier service as needed. The item total across parcels must still match the quantity remaining to ship.
3. Delivery-address changes also update the Sales Order's delivery address after a successful save. This editor does not change products, prices, ordered quantities, payments or stock.
4. An unsubmitted local draft can use a different carrier account and add or remove parcels. A carrier-created shipment keeps its existing account, shipment identity, parcel count and domestic/international destination category. Read the reason shown for a blocked edit; Solvantis does not cancel or replace the carrier shipment automatically.
5. For international shipments, review the customs declaration against the revised parcel contents and confirm non-sale declared values where required. Resolve any missing or restricted-product details before pricing.
6. Refresh shipping prices, select a service and review the proposed price. Confirm the carrier update when requested. **Cancel** discards unsaved edits without changing the shipment.
7. After a carrier-affecting save, generate fresh labels before dispatch. If the previous PDF included several orders, their shared PDF must also be refreshed, although the other orders' shipment details and postage remain unchanged.

> **Important:** Discard every older printed or downloaded copy of an affected label PDF. Solvantis stops offering superseded documents, but cannot remove paper labels or files already downloaded.

Editing is unavailable after dispatch or manifest booking has begun, while carrier or label processing is in progress, or while an outcome requires reconciliation. A shared PDF that includes a dispatched or manifested shipment also blocks an amendment. Carrier-created editing is available only where in-place carrier amendments have been verified. Quotes and recorded carrier charges are displayed separately because final charges can include carrier surcharges. If a save is rejected, correct the reported issue; do not assume the new details were saved. If the result is uncertain, use the indicated reconciliation action before dispatch or another edit. Do not repeatedly submit the change or purchase replacement postage.

To split, consolidate, or reorganise commercial demand, choose **Move items** on an open Sales Order. Select a quantity preset or enter the quantity to move for each outstanding line. For every selected line, also choose how much of its protected incoming supply should follow; this can be zero and cannot exceed either the moved quantity or the protection currently available. Then create a new Sales Order or choose a compatible existing destination and review the complete move before confirming it. If the move takes every outstanding quantity, acknowledge in the review that the source Sales Order will close. Activity History on both orders records the movement and links to the other Sales Order.

To consolidate several open B2B Sales Orders, mark their rows and choose **Move into one order**. Choose which marked order should remain as the destination. Solvantis checks every source against that destination and explains incompatible customer, location, currency, tax, pricing, payment, shipment, or channel details. Review each source's full outstanding quantity and adjust how much protected incoming supply follows each line. After acknowledging that the source orders will close, Solvantis applies the complete batch in one transaction; if any source changed or cannot move, none of the marked orders is changed.

Solvantis creates a saved destination with the source customer, location, commercial terms, delivery details, and customer PO reference, then opens it in the standard Sales Order editor. Review or change the saved order under its normal status rules. A Draft source creates a Draft destination, a Backordered source creates a Backordered destination, and active or In Progress demand creates a Confirmed destination. A Draft destination cannot receive protected incoming supply; set that amount to zero or choose a Confirmed destination.

For existing destinations, Solvantis separates orders that cannot receive the items and shows the reason, including customer, location, currency, tax, payment, shipment, channel, or Xero differences. Customer PO references may differ and remain attached to their respective Sales Orders. A linked Xero invoice does not block the move solely because it exists: Solvantis checks its live status, payments, credits, document date, and organisation lock dates. An unpaid Draft, Submitted, or Authorised invoice outside a locked period can be updated safely; paid, credited, voided, deleted, locked, or unverifiable invoices remain blocked. On confirmation, the ordered quantities and selected incoming protection move together atomically, then Solvantis updates the affected Xero invoices. A post-move Xero failure records a Runtime Issue for reconciliation without repeating the stock movement. Existing fulfilled quantities remain on their original orders, each order keeps its own freight and discount, and no goods are dispatched or stock on hand changed. If either order or its protected supply changed after the review, refresh and review the move again.

> **Important:** If Solvantis reports that the carrier outcome is unknown, do not create another booking. Check the Australia Post portal, then use **Reconcile** with the confirmed carrier order ID. Solvantis verifies that the carrier order contains exactly the expected shipments before enabling the manifest printout.

Prepared work is not lost when the dialog closes. **Prepared & labelled batches** groups the customer orders prepared together, while each nested row remains a separate carrier consignment with its own tracking identity. Choose **Open batch** to continue pricing, carrier submission, label polling, printing, dispatch, or a pending channel retry. **Ready to dispatch** means labels exist but staff have not yet confirmed that the parcels physically left. Use **Delete batch** only for local drafts. A batch that has reached Australia Post cannot be deleted because it may already have incurred postage; continue or resolve it with the carrier instead. 16. To record goods that leave outside Shipping Workspace, select **Fulfil**. Each stock line shows its current on-hand quantity, the quantity ready for this Sales Order, protected incoming supply, current shortfall, and demand priority. Priority follows required date first and then the oldest Sales Order. The quantity starts at the amount ready for this order; enter only what physically leaves in this shipment. 17. Solvantis does not allow one Sales Order to consume received stock protected for another order. When unprotected stock is currently assigned to higher-priority demand, continuing with a lower-priority order requires a reason, which is recorded in Activity History. 18. When every outstanding unit is entered, choose **Fulfil order** to complete the Sales Order. When the entered quantity leaves a remainder, choose **Partially fulfil now** to keep the balance on this order, or **Create a backorder for the remainder** when the supply shortfall needs a separate Sales Order. Reopen a partial order and use **Continue Fulfilment** for a later shipment.

Create early-payment rules under **Settings > Payment Discounts**. On a new Sales Order, **Early-payment discount** shows the selected customer's current default by name. Keep that default, choose any active rule as an order-only override, or choose **No early-payment discount**. An order-only rule can be selected even when the customer has no default. Solvantis saves the rule details and cutoff date on the new Sales Order using its order date. Later changes to the customer or rule do not rewrite the saved order terms.

When adding a payment to a Sales Order with saved early-payment terms, Solvantis previews the discount, qualifying settlement total, cutoff date, and amount still required. The preview includes earlier payments dated on or before the cutoff and the payment currently being entered. A payment after the cutoff does not qualify, and freight is not part of the discount base.

When the entered payment reaches the qualifying settlement, turn on **Apply discount and create the customer credit note** before saving. Solvantis records the payment, a stock-neutral customer credit note, and the discount application together. It does not issue store credit because the credit note settles the order balance. An applied settlement payment and its credit note must be corrected together.

The Sales Order detail view separates the order summary, shipments, products and totals, payments, accounting, stock allocation, and activity with labelled dividers. Each stock product shows on-hand stock, stock ready for this order, protected incoming supply, shortfall, and its demand priority. Confirming an order commits its full stock-tracked demand even when Available becomes negative; incoming allocations protect supply for a named order but do not create a second commitment. **Stock allocation** appears below Accounting. It opens automatically when the order has active allocations and stays collapsed initially when it has none. Its header shows active links, allocated and ready quantities, unsourced demand, and at-risk promises; expand it to allocate incoming supply or manage an existing promise.

Before reverting a Confirmed Sales Order to Draft, expand **Stock allocation** and release any active incoming allocations. Solvantis blocks the reversion while protected Purchase Order supply remains linked, preventing Draft demand from retaining or hiding an active stock promise.

Sales Order accounting shows actual COGS only for shipped units with a positive cost captured at fulfilment. Current Average Cost, falling back to Standard Cost, may be shown separately as an estimate for remaining units; it is not included in actual COGS or gross margin. Gross margin is shown only after every quantity is shipped and its cost is available. If negative stock is explicitly allowed while Average Cost is unavailable, actual COGS remains unresolved. A later receipt does not silently change the earlier shipment's captured cost.

> **Important:** Shopify remains the authority for whether its order was physically fulfilled. Solvantis applies dedicated Shopify fulfilment events, reconciles a fully fulfilled Shopify order update when a dedicated event was missed, and checks the previous 36 hours of fully fulfilled Shopify orders each night. An automatic repair records an operational warning for administrators because the normal event pathway did not bring the Sales Order up to date. Partial Shopify fulfilments remain event-owned and are not inferred by the nightly check. If Shopify reports fulfilment before stock reaches the selected Solvantis location, Solvantis completes it only when recorded incoming purchase-order or branch-transfer stock fully covers the shortage. Stock may temporarily become negative until that supply is received. IMS Notifications names each affected product, fulfilled quantity, stock change, and incoming coverage so staff can complete the pending receipt and verify location stock. An unexplained or only partly covered shortage remains blocked for review.

15. If the remaining quantity will not be shipped as planned, select **Resolve Outstanding** and review the choices below.

| Resolve Outstanding choice   | Use it when                                           | Result                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leave partially open         | A short delay is expected                             | The remainder stays on the current order                |
| Cancel outstanding remainder | The customer no longer wants the balance              | The source order closes at the quantity already shipped |
| Create a backorder for the remainder | The balance is waiting for future supply and needs separate follow-up | Only the unshipped balance moves to a separate Sales Order |

## Worked examples

### Ship an order in two deliveries

A customer orders 10 shirts at $49.95 each, total $499.50 including GST. Ship 6 now, leaving 4 outstanding. The first fulfilment reduces stock by 6. When the last 4 arrive, use **Continue Fulfilment** and ship 4; the first 6 are not moved again.

### Move the supply shortfall to a backorder

An order contains 8 lamps. You dispatch 5 and choose **Create a backorder for the remainder**. Stock reduces by 5, the source order records that shipment, and a separate Backordered Sales Order carries the remaining 3 without another stock movement. Find it later with the **Backordered** status filter and choose **Release backorder** when fulfilment should resume.
